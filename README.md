## 🌟 Executive Overview

Established on **1st February 1884**, **Postal Life Insurance (PLI)** is India’s oldest life insurer, originally instituted for postal employees and later expanded to Central and State Government staff, Defence & Paramilitary forces, Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs), Nationalized Banks, Local Bodies, Educational Institutions, and qualified professionals (Engineers, Doctors, Lawyers, CAs). In 1995, **Rural Postal Life Insurance (RPLI)** was instituted following the Malhotra Committee recommendations to bring sovereign life protection to India's rural populace.

This portal provides an **enterprise-grade, high-precision actuarial calculation and advisory platform**. Re-engineered from official Department of Posts (DoP) actuarial tables, it features exact **Age Next Birthday (ANB)** derivations, multi-mode premium grids with official advance rebates, survival milestone schedules, policy loan forecasters, and direct 1-click advisor connectivity.

## 🧮 Actuarial Calculation Engine & Formulas

The calculation logic conforms strictly to official India Post rules:

### 1. Age as on Next Birthday (ANB)
$$\text{Completed Age} = \text{DateDiffInCompletedYears}(\text{DOB}, \text{EffectiveDate})$$
$$\text{ANB} = \text{Completed Age} + 1$$
*Both Date of Birth and Current Completed Age inputs resolve consistently to the exact official ANB.*

### 2. Base Premium Lookup
$$\text{Base Monthly Premium} = \left(\frac{\text{Sum Assured}}{1000}\right) \times \text{Table Rate}(\text{Age}, \text{Term})$$

- **Table I (Suraksha / Suvidha)**: Indexed by term to ceasing age ($\text{Ceasing Age} - \text{ANB}$).
- **Table II (Santosh)**: Indexed by policy term ($5 \le \text{Term} \le 45$).
- **Table IV (Sumangal)**: 15-Year and 20-Year anticipated endowment rates.
- **Table V (Yugal Suraksha)**: Dual-life joint entry age mortality calibration.
- **Table VI (Bal Jeevan Bima)**: Child entry age rates (5–20).

### 3. High Sum Assured (HSA) Rebate Matrix
- **Single Life (PLI & RPLI)**: **₹1.00 per ₹20,000 Sum Assured**
  $$\text{Monthly Rebate} = \left\lfloor\frac{\text{Sum Assured}}{20000}\right\rfloor \times 1.00$$
  *(₹20K $\to$ ₹1, ₹1L $\to$ ₹5, ₹5L $\to$ ₹25, ₹10L $\to$ ₹50, ₹50L $\to$ ₹250)*
- **Joint Life (Yugal Suraksha)**: **₹1.00 per ₹10,000 Sum Assured**
  $$\text{Monthly Rebate} = \left\lfloor\frac{\text{Sum Assured}}{10000}\right\rfloor \times 1.00$$

### 4. Multi-Mode Installments & Advance Payment Discounts
Official Department of Posts advance payment rebates:
- **Monthly**: $\text{Gross} = \text{Base Monthly Premium}$
- **Quarterly**: $\text{Gross} = \text{Base Monthly} \times 3$
- **Half-Yearly**: $\text{Gross} = (\text{Base Monthly} \times 6) \times (1 - 0.01)$ *(1% advance discount)*
- **Yearly**: $\text{Gross} = (\text{Base Monthly} \times 12) \times (1 - 0.02)$ *(2% advance discount)*

Mode rebates scale proportionally:
$$\text{Installment Rebate} = \text{Monthly Rebate} \times \text{Mode Multiplier (1, 3, 6, 12)}$$
$$\text{Net Installment} = \text{Gross Installment} - \text{Installment Rebate}$$

### 5. Money-Back Survival Milestones (Sumangal)
- **15-Year Term**: 20% at Year 6, 20% at Year 9, 20% at Year 12, 40% + Full Bonus at Year 15.
- **20-Year Term**: 20% at Year 8, 20% at Year 12, 20% at Year 16, 40% + Full Bonus at Year 20.

### 6. Maturity & Accrued Bonus Formulation
$$\text{Total Accrued Bonus} = \left(\frac{\text{Sum Assured}}{1000}\right) \times \text{Bonus Rate} \times \text{Policy Duration}$$
$$\text{Estimated Maturity Benefit} = \text{Sum Assured} + \text{Total Accrued Bonus} + \text{Terminal Bonus}$$

## 🔒 Security & Privacy Posture

The application adheres to OWASP and NIST web security benchmarks:

- **100% Static Client Computation**: All actuarial models execute client-side. Zero server-side API endpoints (`/api/*`) and zero Server Actions (`"use server"`), eliminating SQLi, NoSQLi, and SSRF.
- **HTTP Security Headers ([next.config.ts](file:///Users/pd/Github/pli/next.config.ts))**:
  - `Content-Security-Policy`: Restricts scripts, styles, fonts, images, and embeds to authorized origins.
  - `X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN`: Robust Clickjacking prevention.
  - `Strict-Transport-Security`: HSTS enforced with 2-year duration and preloading.
  - `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`: Enforces strict MIME typing.
  - `Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin`: Guards referrer headers.
  - `Permissions-Policy`: Hardened against unauthorized camera, mic, and geolocation access.
- **Zero PII Persistence**: No customer personal data (DOB, phone number, name) is persisted to `localStorage`, `sessionStorage`, or cookies.
- **Safe External Navigation**: All external links with `target="_blank"` strictly incorporate `rel="noopener noreferrer"`.
- **Zero Secrets**: Automated regex scans confirm zero API keys, secrets, or database tokens in the codebase.
