## 🌟 Executive Overview

Established on **1st February 1884**, **Postal Life Insurance (PLI)** is India’s oldest life insurer. Designed for Central/State Government employees, Defence personnel, PSUs, Banks, and qualified professionals, alongside **Rural Postal Life Insurance (RPLI)** introduced in 1995 for rural citizens.

This portal provides an **enterprise-grade, responsive actuarial engine** replicating official Department of Posts rate tables, high-sum-assured rebate matrices, compound bonus accumulators, and direct advisor WhatsApp connectivity.


## 🧮 Actuarial Calculation Rules

1. **Age Next Birthday (ANB)**:
   $$\text{ANB} = \text{Current Year} - \text{Birth Year} + (\text{if birthday hasn't occurred yet this year then } 1 \text{ else } 0)$$
2. **Gross Premium Formulation**:
   $$\text{Base Monthly Premium} = \left(\frac{\text{Sum Assured}}{1000}\right) \times \text{Table Rate}(\text{Age}, \text{Term})$$
3. **High Sum Assured Rebates**:
   - For $\text{Sum Assured} \ge ₹1,00,000$: Flat rebate of **₹1.00 per ₹1,000 Sum Assured** is deducted.
4. **Declared Bonus Accumulation**:
   $$\text{Accrued Bonus} = \left(\frac{\text{Sum Assured}}{1000}\right) \times \text{Bonus Rate} \times \text{Policy Term (Years)}$$
5. **Maturity Payout**:
   $$\text{Total Maturity Benefit} = \text{Sum Assured} + \text{Accrued Bonus}$$
